## JS demo: things I did not understand

Going through js-demo.html, here are three things I had to look up or still feel fuzzy on:

1. **Arrow functions vs regular functions.** I understand the shorter syntax, but I am not fully clear on when `this` behaves differently between them. The demo used both and I could not tell when to prefer one over the other.

2. **`addEventListener` vs inline `onclick`.** The demo used `addEventListener` but I have also seen `onclick=""` in HTML. I did not understand the difference or why one is preferred. After reading more I learned `addEventListener` keeps JavaScript out of the HTML and allows multiple listeners on the same element.

3. **`JSON.stringify` and `JSON.parse`.** When I logged an object to the output div it just said `[object Object]`. I did not know I needed `JSON.stringify` to turn it into readable text, and I did not fully understand why JavaScript does not just convert it automatically.
