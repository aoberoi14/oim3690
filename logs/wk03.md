## What I Learned

### Working from a real client brief

- Writing the proposal first made the build process much faster since every section already had a purpose
- Using AI to interview me for the PRD helped me think through details I had not considered, like budget ranges and timelines for the contact form

### CSS Custom Properties and Design Systems

- Setting colors, spacing, and radius values once in :root made it easy to keep all four pages consistent
- Changing one variable updates the whole site, which is much faster than editing each page

### Grid and Flexbox in practice

- Used Grid for the five agencies cards and the work thumbnails
- Used Flexbox for the navigation bar and the footer columns
- Learned how grid-template-columns with repeat() controls how many cards show per row, and how media queries collapse that down on mobile

### AI Interactions

- What I asked: To act as a product manager and interview me to turn my proposal into a PRD
- What AI gave me: A series of one-at-a-time questions about the homepage priority, contact form fields, work page format, the five agencies, and the about page
- What I kept or changed: Answered each question based on what I actually know about the agency, then used the resulting PRD.md as the reference for building every page
